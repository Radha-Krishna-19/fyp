1The Team Paper

The first half of the project is written up as a conference-style paper. It is kept exactly as submitted; this page states how its numbers relate to the rest of the site.

TitleTopology-Based Coarse-Graining for Multifractal Spectrum Analysis in Metal-Organic Frameworks (docs/paper/G070_Paper.pdf, 8 pages)
AuthorsThe G070 project team and its guide (names on the Overview page)
What it reportsA pipeline from a live MOFX-DB query to a coarse-grained building-block graph and its influential-node multifractal spectrum. On 76 structures (hypothetical hMOF records returned for a CoRE MOF 2019 query) Δα ranges from 1.032 to 1.582, with an interquartile band of 1.190–1.222.
Limits it statesΔα depends strongly on graph size (0.54 on one cell, 1.18 on a 2×2×2 supercell of the same framework); 17 structures are topological duplicates; individual power-law fits are often poor; no adsorption property has been tested.
Relation to this siteThe paper's numbers come from the independent coarse-grained run (top 20 % influential blocks, radii up to half the diameter), the second of the four estimators recorded in the project notes. Its percentile classes (Narrow ≤ 1.190 < Medium ≤ 1.222 < Wide) are the ones examined in “Sub-bands, second route” on the Results page, where they are shown to follow graph size. The site's own band (interquartile range 1.11–1.25) was computed with a different estimator on the atomic graph and is withdrawn; the two sets of numbers should not be compared directly. The later analyses on this site extend, and do not contradict, its conclusions: the size dependence it reports was confirmed on four nets and on both complete datasets, and Δα was withdrawn as a material property.
