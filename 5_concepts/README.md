# Concepts — read in this order

Five documents covering the project from the chemistry up. Each is self-contained, but they
build on each other in this order.

**Each comes in two formats.** Click the `.md` link to read it here on GitHub; the `.docx` is
the same content as a Word file for printing or submission. Both are generated from one
source, so they cannot drift apart.

| # | Read here | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [MOF and Crystallography Fundamentals](01_MOF_and_Crystallography_Fundamentals.md) | What a MOF is, nodes and linkers, the CIF format, why bonds must be inferred, periodicity, coordination number and topology |
| 2 | [The Published Decomposition Algorithm](02_The_Published_Decomposition_Algorithm.md) | The MOFid pipeline stage by stage, `is_metal()`, the metal-oxo rule, the alternative splits |
| 3 | **[Why This Approach](03_Why_This_Approach.md)** | **Start here if you want the rationale.** Why a graph, why simulation doesn't scale, what the multifractal spectrum measures, what was and wasn't achieved |
| 4 | [Network and Multifractal Analysis](04_Network_and_Multifractal_Analysis.md) | The quotient graph, influential nodes, the Laplacian, the spectrum, and the 77-framework band |
| 5 | [The Website — What and Why](05_The_Website_What_and_Why.md) | Why the site exists, what each section does, what to demonstrate live |

**If you only read one:** Document 3 explains why the project takes the approach it does.
Document 4 contains the analysis and the results.

**If you are presenting:** Document 5 tells you what to click and in what order;
`../PRESENTATION_SCRIPT.md` gives you the words.
