# Concepts — read in this order

Five Word documents covering the whole project, from the chemistry up. Each is
self-contained, but they build on each other in this order.

| # | Document | What it covers | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01_MOF_and_Crystallography_Fundamentals.docx` | What a MOF is, nodes and linkers, the CIF format, why bonds must be inferred rather than read, periodicity and the minimum-image convention, coordination number and topology | 8 |
| 2 | `02_The_Published_Decomposition_Algorithm.docx` | The MOFid pipeline stage by stage, `is_metal()`, the metal-oxo rule, the two alternative splits, MOFid and MOFkey | 7 |

algorithm. Document 4 is the contribution to the analysis extension.
