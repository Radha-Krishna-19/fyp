# Check sheet: independent check of the 16 propositions

Purpose: the propositions on the Maths page (`website/morph_math.html`, source
`website/_build/fragments/morph_math.before.html` and `website/morph_math_page.js`) were written and checked
numerically by one author. Each must be read and checked by a second person.

**How:** for each proposition, (1) restate it in your own words, (2) follow every step of the proof and mark any
step you cannot justify, (3) check one example by hand (for example the numbers in the live check on the page),
(4) record the result. A step you cannot follow is reported, not skipped.

Split: Keerthi checks 1–8, Isha checks 9–16; then each reads two of the other's (suggested: Keerthi 11 and 14,
Isha 5 and 7).
